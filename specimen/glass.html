Legibility

Each panel has a line of text on bare glass and the same text on a plate. The backdrops are stress tests drawn in code: a landscape, a dark ground, fine black and white detail, and white beside black.

What it reveals

Over a live data field the glass shows the field, blurred, and a layer of system lines drawn on the glass itself. Each line is a category with its own line style; every line is also named on a plate.

States

Glass adds no meaning of its own: each cue repeats one the state system already gives. Stale clouds, offline goes solid, maintenance shows its seam and fasteners.

Fallbacks

The same panel, three ways. Reduced transparency and outdoor replace every layer of glass with a solid surface. Print, forced colors and a browser without backdrop-filter do the same.
