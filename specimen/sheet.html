Ruled sheet

A page for writing and reading long text: a margin, ruled lines and an order of parts.

SystemLightDarkOutdoorRight to left

Reading

One page per day. Each entry is one fixed-height ruled line: the asset in the margin, the subject on the line, its state at the end. Select a line to read it in the pane; a line never grows in place.

Press N for a new entry.

Writing

New entry opens the workspace: a side panel over a scrim with the setup in one bar, the ruled sheet as the page, and one filled action in the footer. Changes stay on this device as a draft until the entry is created.

Unfinished draft restored.
