What the dark is for

It is easy to think of a light as something that arrives at the end: the room is dark, then it is lit, and the work is finished. Most evenings are not like that. The light is low from the start, and it stays low, and you read by it anyway.

There is a particular kind of attention that only comes when nothing is being asked of you. No one is waiting for the answer. A paragraph can be read three times, and the third time it is a different paragraph. Nothing is lost by going slowly.

Entropy is a word that stays vague until the evening you try to explain it to yourself without the book. It is a measure of how many ways a thing can be arranged without anyone being able to tell. You are not behind. You are somewhere in the middle of it.

Field

What you know, wonder and have noted, laid out however you like. Dark is where you have not been yet, and it is not a gap.
